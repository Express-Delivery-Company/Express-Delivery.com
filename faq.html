Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about shipment requests, tracking, and package handling.

How long does delivery take?

Delivery time depends on the route, service type, customs requirements, and package handling needs. Express Air is usually faster than standard shipping.

How do I track my shipment?

Go to the Track Shipment page, enter your Order ID and email address, then view the tracking map and route history.

Can I ship fragile goods?

Yes. Include fragile handling notes in your goods description so the team can advise on packaging and route requirements.

Is this connected to live GPS?

The current tracking page can later be connected to a database, customer accounts, payment, and real-time GPS updates.

What information should I include in a shipment request?

Include pickup country, destination country, package type, quantity, weight estimate, fragile notes, and clear packaging details when possible.

Why might a shipment be held?

Customs checks, incomplete documentation, restricted goods, route delays, or carrier inspections can pause a shipment while the issue is reviewed.

Can support update my shipment details?

Yes. Contact support with your Order ID, email address, and the detail that needs correction so the team can review it.
